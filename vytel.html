The continuation of CuroNexus

CuroNexus connects the care. Vytel makes sure the tests actually happen.

Doctors advise tests every day, and many never get done: the lab is far, the queue is long, the patient is unwell or elderly. Vytel removes the trip. Same company, same privacy standards, same goal: a patient journey that doesn’t break between steps.

How it works

Four steps, and you never leave home.

01Book

Pick tests and a slot

Choose a test or health package, or upload a prescription. Add family members and pick a morning slot that suits fasting tests.

02Track

See who’s coming

Get your collector’s name and number, and follow them live on the map until they reach your door.

03Collect

A quick, safe draw

The collector confirms your OTP, draws the sample into barcoded tubes, and carries it in a temperature-checked cold box.

04Report

Results on your phone

A NABL-accredited partner lab processes the sample and the report comes to you, and to your doctor if you choose.

Every booking, at a glance

Follow your sample like a parcel.

Inside Vytel, every booking is shown as a sample tube. The cap colour tells you the status; the fill tells you how far the collection has got. Patients, collectors and our operations team all see the same picture.

Chain of custody

Every tube accounted for, from your arm to the analyser.

A lost or mislabelled sample means another needle. Vytel records each hand-off so we always know where a sample is and who has it.

Patient OTP check

The collector can only start once the patient’s one-time code matches the booking.

Barcoded tubes

Each tube is scanned to the booking at collection, and scanned again at the lab.

Cold-box temperature

Collectors record cold-box temperature so samples stay within the lab’s limits.

Photo proof and lab hand-over

Proof photos at collection and a signed hand-over at the lab close the record.

Works offline

The collector app queues actions without signal and syncs when the network returns, so nothing is lost in a basement or a lift.
