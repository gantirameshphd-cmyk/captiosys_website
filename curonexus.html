Referrals

A referral is a promise. CuroNexus tracks it to the end.

Every referral moves through a defined lifecycle. Both doctors see where it stands, and the patient gets the referred doctor’s contact card (name, specialisation, clinic, address, phone and map link) in every notification.

Unanswered referrals expire automatically after 48 hours, so the referring doctor knows to act instead of waiting.

draft→ sent→ acknowledged→ accepted→ in_treatment→ completed
or acknowledged→declined · sent→expiredafter 48 h
Architecture

Eight layers, each doing one job.

CuroNexus runs on AWS in the Mumbai region with disaster recovery in Singapore. Services talk to each other over mutual TLS, and every layer can scale on its own.

01ClientsDoctor portal, patient portal, admin console (React 18), mobile app in phase 2
02EdgeCloudFront CDN, WAF with OWASP rules, Route 53 failover, managed TLS certificates
03API gatewayJWT validation, per-role rate limits, path-based routing
04Microservices8 NestJS services on ECS Fargate, mutual TLS between them
05DataPostgreSQL Multi-AZ, Redis 7, encrypted S3 with cross-region replication
06Search & eventsOpenSearch for doctor discovery, SQS/SNS and EventBridge for async workflows
07NotificationsEmail, SMS and push fan-out with delivery logging
08ComplianceAES-256, TLS 1.3, RBAC, append-only audit log, DPDP Act 2023

Security & compliance

Health data treated as the most sensitive data there is.

Encrypted everywhere

AES-256 at rest with customer-managed keys, TLS 1.3 in transit, mutual TLS between services.

Consent-first access

Patients grant and withdraw access per doctor, aligned to the DPDP Act 2023 and HIPAA-aligned practices.

Append-only audit trail

Every read of a health record is logged with who, when and why. Health records are never hard-deleted.

Strong sign-in

Doctors sign in with password plus a 6-digit authenticator code; patients with a mobile OTP.

When the doctor advises tests, Vytel picks up.

CuroNexus handles the clinical side: consult, prescription, referral. Vytel is how the advised tests actually get done, with a trained collector at the patient’s home and the report coming back to the doctor.
