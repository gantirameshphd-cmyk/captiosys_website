About Captiosys

From building for others to building our own.

Captiosys Micro Technologies Private Limited was founded in Hyderabad in 2013. We spent our first decade in software services and IoT engineering. Today we build healthcare products: CuroNexus and Vytel.

Our story

Three chapters so far.

2013 onwards

Software services

  • Incorporated in Hyderabad
  • Custom software and systems for clients
IoT years

Devices & data

  • An IoT engineering group building connected hardware and edge software
  • Deep experience in data pipelines and cloud platforms
Today

Healthcare products

  • CuroNexus: connected care for doctors, patients and referrals
  • Vytel: home sample collection, the continuation of CuroNexus
What we believe

Good care depends on the steps between appointments.

Continuity over features

We measure ourselves by whether the patient’s journey holds together, from consult to referral to test to report.

Privacy is the product

Patients decide who sees their data. We design for that from the first table we create.

India first

Built for Indian cities, Indian regulation and Indian patients, from mobile-OTP sign-in to DLT-registered SMS.
