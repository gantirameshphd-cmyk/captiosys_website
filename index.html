How they connect

From the consulting room to the lab report, without the patient falling through.

A doctor advises tests. Too often the patient never gets them done, or the report never makes it back. CuroNexus and Vytel are designed as two halves of the same journey.

01 CuroNexus

Consult & record

The doctor sees the patient, writes the prescription and keeps notes and records in one consented patient profile.

02 CuroNexus

Refer or order tests

Refer to a specialist with a tracked referral, or advise diagnostic tests the patient can book in a tap.

03 Vytel

Collect at home

A Vytel collector arrives in the booked slot, verifies the patient, scans barcoded tubes and hands them to a partner lab.

04 Both

Report back

The lab uploads the report. The patient gets it on their phone and, with consent, it reaches the doctor who asked for it.

Doctors already use Vytel today through a personal booking link and QR code. Direct test orders from CuroNexus into Vytel are on our roadmap.

Why Captiosys

Healthcare software that behaves like infrastructure.

Health data is personal, and a missed sample means a repeat needle. We build for both: strict on privacy, obsessive about the last mile.

Privacy built in, not bolted on

Consent capture, data-principal rights and audit logging designed around India’s DPDP Act 2023. Encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access everywhere.

Cloud-native, India-hosted

NestJS services, React and React Native clients, PostgreSQL and Redis on AWS in Mumbai, provisioned with Terraform and shipped through CI/CD.

We run the last mile ourselves

Vytel collectors are our own fleet, not a marketplace. That’s how we can promise a slot, show live tracking and keep a clean chain of custody for every tube.

Engineering since 2013

Captiosys started in software services and IoT. Those years of building and running systems for others now go into our own products.
